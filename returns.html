Return Policy

Cancellations and address changes

You can cancel your order or change the shipping address within 2 hours of placing it for a full refund. After that your print is already in production and the order can't be changed or cancelled.

Damaged, defective or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged, has a printing defect, or isn't what you ordered, email me within 14 days of delivery with your order number and photos of the item and its packaging. I'll send a free replacement or give you a full refund — your choice.

Lost parcels

If your order hasn't arrived 60 days after you placed it and the tracking doesn't show it as delivered, let me know and I'll send a free replacement or a full refund. If tracking shows the parcel as delivered but you can't find it, please check with neighbours, your building's front desk and the carrier first; I'll replace it once the carrier confirms it's lost.

Wrong address, refused or unclaimed parcels

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If a parcel comes back because the address was incomplete or incorrect, or it was refused or not collected, I can resend it once the reshipping cost is covered, or refund the order minus the production and shipping costs already paid.

Change of mind

As each piece is made to order, I can't offer returns or exchanges if you change your mind or choose a different size or material. Please check the details before you order. Colours on screen can look slightly different from a printed photograph depending on your display, and this isn't considered a defect.

Customs and import duties

Orders shipped across borders may be charged import duties or taxes by the destination country. These are paid by the recipient and aren't refundable. Orders to the United States ship from a US print lab.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to your original payment method through PayPal, usually within 14 days of approval. Your bank or card issuer may take a few more days to show it.

Contact

Reply to your order confirmation email, or write to billlin914@gmail.com with your order number, and I'll get back to you as soon as I can.

This policy does not affect your statutory rights as a consumer.
